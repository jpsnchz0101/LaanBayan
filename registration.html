Republic Act No. 10173

Privacy and Policy

Barangay Ugac Sur is committed to respecting and safeguarding the personal data and privacy of all residents in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When registering an account or applying for barangay services through the Laan Bayan portal, we collect essential resident identification details including:

  • Full name and age
  • Residential address, zone, and street
  • Duration of residency in your Barangay
  • Active mobile telephone contact number
  • Account credentials (encrypted password)
  • Requested civic documents, transaction purpose, and appointment schedules

2. Purpose of Collection & Processing

The personal data collected is processed strictly for the following legitimate civic and administrative frontline services:

  • Verifying residency status and resident identity records.
  • Scheduling appointments and assigning official queue slots for frontline desk services.
  • Validating and issuing requested clearances, certificates, and endorsements.
  • Sending SMS updates or notifications regarding queue numbers and processing schedules.

3. Protection, Confidentiality & Storage

We implement organizational, physical, and technical safeguards to secure your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or unlawful destruction. Access is strictly restricted to authorized barangay personnel and desk officers. We do not sell, disclose, or share personal data with unauthorized third parties or commercial entities.

4. Resident Rights under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject, you retain full rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012:

  • Right to be Informed: To know what personal data is gathered, processed, and protected.
  • Right to Access: To request a summary or verification of your personal records.
  • Right to Rectification: To correct inaccurate or outdated information in your profile.
  • Right to Object or Erasure: To request account deactivation or removal, subject to statutory government archiving laws.

5. Data Protection Inquiries & Contact Desk

For questions, feedback, or requests regarding your personal data or privacy rights, please contact our designated office:

Email: dpo.ugac@laanbayan.gov.ph
Frontline Office: Barangay Hall of Ugac Sur, Tuguegarao City, Cagayan
