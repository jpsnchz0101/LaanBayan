Mission & Purpose

Laan Bayan is a barangay appointment and service availability system built to cut down on wasted trips and long walk-in lines. Residents can check what services are open, book a time slot, and track their place in the queue from anywhere — no more guessing whether the office is even open today.

What we're solving

Long unplanned queues at the barangay hall, unclear service hours, and no way to know ahead of time whether a request can even be processed that day.

Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about online barangay appointments, document processing, and queue policies.

Log in with your contact number or resident name using the portal. Once logged in, select your requested service (e.g., Barangay Clearance or Certificate of Residency), check the list of requirements, choose your preferred date and time slot (morning or afternoon), and click confirm. You will receive an instant digital appointment pass with your queue number.

Yes, walk-ins are always welcomed at Barangay Ugac Sur. However, online appointments are given dedicated queue priority slots. Booking online ensures you have all required documents beforehand and prevents long waiting times at the hall.

Yes! In accordance with Republic Act No. 11261 (First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act), all clearances, certificates, and endorsements issued to first-time jobseekers are 100% free of charge. Simply bring your Barangay Certification of First-Time Jobseeker and valid ID/school ID.

You can monitor live queue movement right from your phone through the resident dashboard. In addition, the barangay hall displays the current serving number on the digital queue monitor at Desk 01 and Desk 02.

Appointments include a 15-minute grace period. If you arrive past your time slot, your pass will remain active in the priority standby pool, and the frontline officer will attend to you during the next open window.

Please bring at least one valid government-issued ID (or student ID for minors/students), proof of residency (such as utility bills or lease agreements), and any service-specific documents listed under our Services section.
